## CommentSold at a glance

CommentSold positions itself as a live-retail system for boutiques rather than a general streaming CDN. Its pricing page includes a website builder, a branded iOS or Android live-selling app, live streaming hours, social selling across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, shoppable Popclips, inventory, invoicing, shipping, and support. That package suits a merchant that needs selling operations tied to the live event, not just a player embedded on an existing enterprise storefront.

The live-commerce role is direct selling: a retailer runs a live sale, viewers buy through configured surfaces, and the operator can publish replays to the webstore or app. Help material describes reporting on viewer counts, revenue from live sales and replays, product performance, and minute-by-minute sales. Those operational details distinguish live retail workflow from an ordinary social broadcast.

## The AI function, stated narrowly

AI ClipHero is the documented AI capability that earns this profile its category placement. CommentSold says the tool analyzes a completed livestream, identifies engaging or product-relevant sections, generates short clips, and creates captions using speech recognition. The help guide explains that an authorized user selects a past live sale, requests AI clips, then reviews, approves, rejects, edits, or downloads results. Approved clips can feed later shoppable Clips and external social content.

This is AI-assisted post-production and repurposing, not an AI host, a real-time live answer engine, or footage automatically generated during a sale. The vendor warns generation can take 15 minutes to several hours, depending on replay duration, products, and the request queue. Plan it as a back-office workflow with an editorial reviewer, not as a function that creates a clip at the moment a host shows an item. Human approval is an important control for price claims, product safety, and captions.

## Commercial and implementation constraints

The published table lists Starter at $149 per month plus 6% of sales, Small Business at $499 plus 5%, and Large Business at $999 plus 4%. It advertises a 15-day free trial and lists AI ClipHero across displayed tiers. Check the figures at purchase because live hours, payment processing, sales percentage, onboarding, channel privileges, and local commerce rules can affect the agreement.

The reviewed official pages establish social channels and native web or mobile commerce surfaces, but do not establish public developer API documentation. This profile deliberately marks API availability as unknown rather than inferring it from a dashboard or mobile app. A merchant requiring headless checkout, event automation, data-export endpoints, or bespoke identity integration should obtain written technical confirmation before selecting it.

Run a pilot from the actual catalog: validate SKUs, inventory, tax, invoices, fulfillment, moderation, and channel policies while a live sale is running. After the event, request clips, inspect selection and captions, approve only accurate items, and verify that Popclips link to live products. Avoid publishing an automatically selected highlight without checking whether original price, availability, or offer window changed.

Choose CommentSold when integrated boutique operations and replay-to-shoppable-clip reuse are central. Compare Firework or Bambuser for enterprise player and commerce integration, buywith for influencer-led shared shopping sessions, and Videowise for modular AI clips and live distribution. The decisive AI feature is ClipHero's bounded replay processing, not the fact that the platform sells through video.

Store each approved clip with its source sale, product identifier, approval date, and expiration check. That small audit trail makes it easier to withdraw a stale offer and explain merchandising decisions later.
